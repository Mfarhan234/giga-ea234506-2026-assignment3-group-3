# Contributions

Each member adds **their own line**, committed **from their own GitHub account**.
Add your line on the first day, then update it before the final deadline to say what you actually did.
Copy the final version into Appendix A of the report.

Every member runs or observes at least one field session. Your GitHub username appears in the
`team_present` column of `evaluation/data/sessions.csv` for the sessions you were at.

Format: `- Full Name · NRP · GitHub username · what I did`
